Publish your own artwork once, to your own channels.

Wallpaper Studio Publisher is a desktop publishing tool for independent wallpaper artists and small design studios. You write one post by hand and it goes to the accounts you own — without retyping and re-uploading the same work five times.

How it works

  1. Connect your own accounts. You authorize each platform through its official sign-in screen. The app never sees your password, and it can only reach accounts you personally authorize.
  2. Compose one post. Type a title, a caption and hashtags, then attach your own artwork or a video you produced. Per-channel overrides let you tweak wording where a platform needs it.
  3. Preview, then publish. A dry run shows exactly what will be sent to each platform before anything leaves your machine. Publish immediately, or queue the post for a sensible hour.

Supported channels

  • TikTok

    Video and photo posts. Publish directly, or send to your TikTok drafts and finish in the app.

  • YouTube

    Videos and Shorts, with native scheduling and privacy control.

  • Instagram

    Feed posts, Reels and Stories on a Business or Creator account.

  • Facebook

    Page posts, photo albums and Reels.

  • Telegram

    Channel posts and albums, with the option to keep files at original quality.

It runs on your computer

There is no hosted service behind this app and no account to create with us. You run it on your own machine, and everything it knows stays in a folder next to it: your access tokens, your drafts, your media files. We operate no servers, receive no telemetry, and have no way to see your content or reach your accounts.

One creator, one installation. The app has no multi-account management, no content scraping, no bulk import, and no automation of follows, likes, comments or messages. It publishes work you made, to accounts you own, when you press publish.

Who it's for

Artists who finish a wallpaper set, record a short walkthrough, and want it on their channels without an afternoon of copy-pasting. Typical use is a few posts a week — one per finished piece of work.

Getting it

The app is open source under the MIT License. It needs Node.js 20.11 or newer, and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.
